Column reference

Sales (required): order_id, order_date, customer_id, customer_name, product_id, product_name, category, quantity, unit_price, unit_cost

Sales (optional): ship_date, status (Delivered / Shipped / Cancelled / Returned), ship_mode, payment_method, gender, age, city, state, region, segment, sub_category, discount (0.15 or 15)

Products (required): product_id, name, category, unit_cost, unit_price · optional: sub_category, stock_qty, reorder_level, supplier

Dates: YYYY-MM-DD preferred; DD-MM-YYYY is also accepted. Revenue is calculated as quantity x unit_price x (1 - discount); profit = revenue - quantity x unit_cost.
