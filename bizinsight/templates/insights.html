How insights are generated

Each rule compares an aggregate against a business threshold - for example "revenue changed by more than 5% versus the previous period", "discounts above 30% have a negative margin", "a class-A product is below its reorder point" or "RFM recency score is 1-2 with frequency score 3+". No predictive models are trained; the rules live in bizinsight/analytics/insights.py.
